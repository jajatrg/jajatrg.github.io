**Tools:** Excel

## Project

**Agroloop** is a startup project for an innovative **organic fertiliser** that reuses
**10,000 tonnes of waste per year**. We reached the **semi-finals** of the emlyon startup
competition.

## My contribution

- Built the **forecast financial model** in Excel: revenue projections, break-even point,
  margin of safety.
- Defined and tracked the project's **KPIs**.
- Contributed to the business plan.

## What I learned

- How to translate a business idea into numbers and assumptions that can be challenged.
