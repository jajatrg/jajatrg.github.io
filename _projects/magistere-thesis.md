**Context:** Magistère d'économie, Université Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne · 2025 · team of 3

**Tools:** R, statistics, econometrics

## Question

Study the **conditions of detention of prisoners** using public data.

## Approach

- Collected and cleaned the data. Many values were missing, so I used **interpolation** to fill
  the gaps in a consistent way.
- Analysed the data in **R** and built the charts.
- Wrote the thesis with two other students.

## What I learned

- Real-world data is rarely complete: cleaning is a large part of the work.
- How to justify methodological choices (like interpolation) and explain their limits.
