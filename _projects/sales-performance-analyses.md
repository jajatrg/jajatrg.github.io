**Context:** Kenvue, pharmacy channel · 2026

**Tools:** SQL (Snowflake), Excel, Power BI

## Brand vs. segment: using data to convince

My manager was not sure that **Microlax** was gaining market share. I compared its growth with
the growth of its segment and showed that the brand was **outperforming its segment**.
The analysis was then **shared with all the teams**.

## Anomaly detection

While comparing year-to-date sales with the previous year (N−1) and month-over-month growth,
I spotted **a pharmacy with an unusual pattern of group discounts**. I documented the anomaly and
escalated it.

## Monthly insights

I wanted to share insights every week. My manager did not have time for that, so after discussing
her constraints we agreed on **monthly insights**: less frequent, but more useful.

## What I learned

- Unexpected findings often come from simply comparing periods carefully.
- Adapting the format to the audience matters as much as the analysis itself.
